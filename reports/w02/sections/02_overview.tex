\section{Overview and Week 02 Objectives}
\label{sec:overview}

\subsection{Context and project goals}
In the \textbf{AI VIET NAM (AIO2026) Topic Team} program, Weeks 02 and 03 focus on running experiments and gathering results. Our project aims to improve data efficiency in Vision MLPs using structural re-parameterization. 

Before testing any new architectures, we first need to implement and test the baseline models properly under the exact same conditions. Therefore, our focus for \textbf{Week 02} is simply \textbf{replicating baselines and standardizing experiments}.

\subsection{Week 02 main objectives}
To build a solid starting point for our project, our team focused on three key goals in Week 02:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{\arabic*.}]
    \item \textbf{Replicating baseline architectures:} Implement two representative Vision MLP models from scratch:
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textbf{ResMLP}~\cite{touvron2021resmlp}: A simple columnar architecture where token mixing uses a single linear layer without non-linear activations.
        \item \textbf{RepMLP}~\cite{ding2022repmlp}: A multi-stage architecture that adds parallel depthwise conv layers during training to inject local image details.
    \end{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Standardizing parameter budgets:} Keep both models at approximately $\approx \mathbf{2.16\text{M} - 2.19\text{M}}$ parameters so that the comparison on \textbf{CIFAR-100} is fair. We also write down the exact closed-form formulas for their parameter counts.
    \item \textbf{Setting up a shared training pipeline:} Train both models for 100 epochs from scratch with identical hyperparameters, data augmentations (RandAugment, Mixup, CutMix), and FP16 mixed precision, recording their loss and accuracy curves.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Scope of this report}
Although we have started coding other experimental variants in our repository (such as local stems, pyramid ResMLP, and our proposed Rep-ResMixer), we deliberately keep them out of this Week 02 report. 

Our team wants to first confirm the baseline results and ensure all training logs are solid before evaluating new modifications in Week 03.
